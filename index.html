How to play

Goal: be the first to fill all six of your slots (1–6) with Dream or REM cards.

On your turn do exactly one thing:

  • Draw the top card of the pile (only with fewer than 8 cards in hand). Its back shows whether it's a Dream or a Nightmare.
  • Dream — play it on your own slot with the same number. It can cover an empty slot, a Nightmare, or a duplicate Dream. A Jolly ↻ fits any slot.
  • REM — play it on any of your empty or Nightmare slots. It counts as a Dream, and Nightmares can't touch it.
  • REM swap — play a matching Dream (or Dream Jolly) onto your opponent's REM: your Dream stays there, and you take their REM into your hand.
  • Nightmare — play it on an opponent's Dream with the same number (Jolly ↻: any Dream). You steal that Dream into your hand and the Nightmare stays in their slot.
  • Discard — with 8 cards in hand you may discard one card instead.

Tap a card then a glowing slot, or drag the card there. Tapping a card that has only one place to go plays it at once. Tap the draw pile to draw.
